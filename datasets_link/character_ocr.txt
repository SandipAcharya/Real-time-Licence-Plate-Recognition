The kaggle link for training and testing data :
"https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/inspiring-lab/nepali-number-plate-characters-dataset"

The dataset contains the 34 different Nepali characters images present in the number plates of Nepali Vehicles. It contains a total of 26,537 images of characters.
